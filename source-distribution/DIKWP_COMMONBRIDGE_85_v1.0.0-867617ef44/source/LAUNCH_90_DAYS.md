# 90-day public launch

## Days 0-7

Publish and pin one repository. Demonstrate a single cross-access collaboration: a manual contributor adds local terms, a local model structures differences, an advanced model proposes a synthesis, and an independent human closes the world-effect record.

## Days 8-30

Recruit 20 external contributors across languages and access tiers. Publish open tasks, not invitations to understand the entire portfolio. Record failures and correction time.

## Days 31-60

Run three bounded pilots: open research, local-language public knowledge and open-source maintenance. Do not enter medical, legal, financial or employment decisions.

## Days 61-90

Publish an evidence report covering participation by tier, manual-path completion, provenance preservation, attribution disputes, true-value observations, maintainer time returned and negative results.

Success is not stars or receipt count. Success is a real contribution from a person who would otherwise have been excluded, with preserved origin and observed value.
