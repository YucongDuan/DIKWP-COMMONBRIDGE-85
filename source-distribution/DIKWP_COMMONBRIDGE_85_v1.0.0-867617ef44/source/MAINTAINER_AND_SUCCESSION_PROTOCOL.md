# Maintainer and Succession Protocol

COMMONBRIDGE-85 is designed to reduce founder dependence rather than create another permanent support burden.

## Roles

- Origin steward: protects DIKWP origin and source continuity.
- Runtime maintainer: reviews code, tests and releases.
- Community steward: keeps manual and low-resource participation usable.
- Policy-profile reviewer: updates jurisdiction and platform profiles without claiming legal authority.
- True-value auditor: verifies that receipts are based on observed effects and never become person scores.
- Independent critic: can reopen claims, challenge routing and request correction.

## Merge rules

A change may merge only when it preserves:

- no person grading;
- no control circumvention;
- manual fallback;
- source and version provenance;
- non-financial true value;
- affected-party voice;
- correction and revocation.

## Founder-energy rule

No feature is accepted solely because it is intellectually interesting. It must have at least one external owner, adopter, dataset, standard interface, funded need or measurable burden-reduction effect.

## Succession

A fork may change goals, but must retain its origin record and use a distinct identity. A successor cannot claim to be the original project or represent Yucong Duan without explicit authorization.
