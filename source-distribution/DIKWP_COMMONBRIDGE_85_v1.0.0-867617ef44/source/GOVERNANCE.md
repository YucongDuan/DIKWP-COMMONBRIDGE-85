# Governance

## Non-negotiable constitutional boundaries

1. Tasks and environments may be classified; persons may not be permanently graded.
2. Lawful fallback is required; circumvention is prohibited.
3. Provenance and disagreement remain visible.
4. True-value receipts remain non-financial and scope-limited.
5. Affected parties may dispute use, attribution and claimed effect.
6. High-impact decisions require qualified, named real-world authority.
7. The founder or any maintainer may not exempt their own actions from audit and correction.

## Roles

- Protocol steward: protects schemas and invariants.
- Maintainer: reviews code and releases.
- Local-context steward: protects regional and community semantics.
- Independent critic: tests claims and misuse resistance.
- Affected-party representative: reviews real-world burdens and appeal paths.

No single role may create a person score, grant external enforcement authority or issue a financial token.
