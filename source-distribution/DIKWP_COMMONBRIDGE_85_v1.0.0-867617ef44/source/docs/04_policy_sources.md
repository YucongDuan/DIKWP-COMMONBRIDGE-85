# Current public policy sources used for design context

Snapshot date: 18 August 2026.

- European Commission, AI Act enters into force: risk-based categories, transparency obligations, high-risk requirements and prohibition of certain social-scoring uses. https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/ai-act-enters-force-2024-08-01_en
- National Internet Information Office of China, 2025/2026 public generative-AI service filing announcements. https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-05/13/c_1780413225190669.htm
- China, Measures for Labelling AI-Generated Synthetic Content and supporting mandatory standard, effective 1 September 2025. https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654685899683.htm
- US Bureau of Industry and Security, current Export Administration Regulations Parts 734, 744 and 774, including advanced-computing/end-use controls. https://www.bis.gov/regulations/ear/744
- US BIS announcement that the January 2025 AI Diffusion Rule was rescinded, while chip-related controls continued and a replacement was planned. https://www.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-announces-rescission-biden-era-artificial-intelligence-diffusion-rule-strengthens
- UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence: human rights, dignity, inclusion, transparency, accountability and environmental sustainability. https://www.unesco.org/en/artificial-intelligence/recommendation-ethics

These examples motivate capability fragmentation and transparency requirements. They do not form a complete legal profile for any user or jurisdiction.
