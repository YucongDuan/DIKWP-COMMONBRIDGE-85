# First public pilot

The recommended pilot is not censorship circumvention. It is a lawful, low-risk collaboration across unequal AI access:

- one contributor with no AI records local observations and terminology;
- one contributor with a small local model structures the differences;
- one contributor with hosted AI proposes a bounded synthesis;
- one independent human reviewer checks decisive claims;
- affected contributors confirm whether local meaning was preserved;
- the system records observed benefit, burden and correction.

Primary success measure: a person who lacked advanced AI access produces a recognized, verifiable contribution without surrendering raw private data or being assigned a lower human rank.
