# Public launch text

AI access is becoming uneven. One person has no model. Another has a small local model. A third has a limited cloud service. An institution has agents, tools and data. This must not become a permanent hierarchy of who is allowed to contribute to knowledge.

DIKWP COMMONBRIDGE-85 compiles one cooperation goal into lawful manual, local, hosted and institutional paths. It preserves provenance, local differences, contribution rights and non-monetary true value. It does not bypass controls and it never grades people.

**Any person. Any lawful AI tier. One verifiable contribution.**
